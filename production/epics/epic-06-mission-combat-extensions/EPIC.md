## Overview

This epic covers the two Feature-layer systems that extend live-combat gameplay on top of the already-Complete Core Combat Engine (Epic 02): **Constellation** (geometric tower-placement patterns that grant stat bonuses and a permanent Discovery Ledger of runes) and **Tower Mastery** (a live proximity accumulator that lets the hero learn permanent skills by standing near towers during combat). Both are bundled here because they're the two Mission-context systems whose entire mechanic is reading live Grid/Tower state during `WAVE_ACTIVE` — they share the same class of performance concern (per-event vs. per-frame spatial queries) and the same cross-system data dependency on Tower System's component loadout, resolved by the identical architectural pattern (extending `tower_placed`'s event payload rather than adding a new query API) in both of their governing ADRs.

**Tower Mastery is flagged HIGH-risk in `systems-index.md`'s own risk table** (proximity-based learning during live combat is untested in the tower-defense genre) — `systems-index.md` explicitly recommends prototyping proximity detection in isolation, and playtesting specifically for "does the mechanic feel fair," before full mission integration. This should happen before or alongside this epic's Tower Mastery stories, not deferred past them.
