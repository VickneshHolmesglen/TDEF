User commissions adversarial economy reviews where the goal is to find problems, not validate decisions.
Expects: numbered findings, explicit math showing the dominant strategy, risk severity rating, and actionable resolution options.
Does not want hedged language like "might be a problem" — wants "this IS a dominant strategy because [math]."

**Why:** The review brief explicitly said "your job is NOT to validate — your job is to find economy design problems."
**How to apply:** Lead with the finding and the math. Rate severity. Offer fix options at the end.
