Vicknesh is the creative director and primary designer on Sanctuary Defenders. He co-authors all GDDs with Claude Sonnet (design/architecture/planning track) and delegates coding tasks to Qwen.

His collaboration pattern with audio-director is adversarial review: he provides a complete audio spec block and asks for problems to be found, not validated. He expects issues tagged [BLOCKING] or [RECOMMENDED] with clear reasoning, not vague concerns.

He is technically literate — references to mix hierarchy, LUFS targets, polyphony voice-steal rules, and WCAG accessibility requirements do not need to be explained from scratch.
