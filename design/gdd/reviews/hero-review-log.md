## Cross-Doc Correction — 2026-07-15 — Formula 4/6 Ownership Ambiguity (+ Formula 5, found incidentally)
`/review-all-gdds` (2026-07-15) flagged Formula 4 as duplicating D&S's Formula D2 (double-multiplication risk, never marked superseded) and Formula 6 as using a stale pre-overkill-cap lifesteal basis vs. D&S's authoritative `absorbed`. Investigated both against the actual D&S pipeline (`damage-status.md`) rather than assuming a simple relabel:

**Formula 4**: Rule 6 (Group 2, already correct and unambiguous) states Hero emits `HeroAutoAttackEvent.base_damage` as the *unmultiplied* effective damage and explicitly says "the Hero System does not resolve the final damage value itself." Formula 4, however, computed a fully-multiplied `final_damage` (applying both the elite/boss tier multiplier and the crit multiplier) with no annotation distinguishing it from what Hero actually sends — a real risk that an implementer reading Formula 4 in isolation, without cross-referencing Rule 6, could populate `base_damage` with this already-multiplied value, causing D&S's Pipeline III to apply both multipliers a second time. Also found: Formula 4's `elite_boss_multiplier` (documented here as a fixed `{1.0, 1.5}` enum) is the same value as D&S's `hero_damage_bonus_multiplier` (documented there as a continuously tunable `[1.25, 2.00]` constant, default 1.50, explicitly D&S-owned in its own Tuning Knobs table) — two GDDs independently defining the same constant under different names and different value models is itself a latent conflict. Fixed: Formula 4 reframed as a full-pipeline *reference* (spans both systems, useful for balance tuning) with an explicit ownership split table, `elite_boss_multiplier` renamed to `hero_damage_bonus_multiplier` matching D&S's owned name, and a correction note pointing to Rule 6 and D&S's Formula D2 as the two authoritative sources.

**Formula 6**: computed `hp_recovered = damage_dealt × effective_lifesteal` using `damage_dealt` = "Formula 4 output" (the raw resolved damage, pre-overkill-cap). D&S's actual lifesteal resolution (Rule VI.4) uses `heal_amount = floor(absorbed × hero_lifesteal_fraction)`, where `absorbed = min(amount_received, pre_hit_hp)` — the real HP removed, capped at the target's remaining HP. On any overkill hit (a very common case — killing blows routinely exceed the target's remaining HP), the old formula would over-heal relative to the actual implementation. Fixed: Formula 6 rewritten to use `absorbed` as the basis, `effective_lifesteal` renamed to `hero_lifesteal_fraction` (matching the actual `HeroAutoAttackEvent` payload field), and a worked example added showing the old formula's over-heal on a concrete overkill case (270 raw damage vs. 180 HP remaining → old formula healed for 54, correct basis heals for 36).

**Formula 5 (found while fixing the above, not originally flagged)**: same disease — duplicated D&S's incoming-hero-damage armor formula (`damage-status.md` Rule V) without its `max(1, ...)` floor guard (D&S's version guarantees no hit is fully negated by armor; this GDD's copy had no such floor), and its "Incoming damage" variable description cited "Formula 4 output" — which is Hero's own *outgoing* auto-attack damage, unrelated to an *incoming* attack against the hero. Fixed with the same ownership-split pattern as Formula 4, floor guard added, and the mislabeled cross-reference corrected. This pipeline remains scaffolding-only at MVP (D&S's Rule V notes no active hero-incoming-damage source exists yet), so the fix is documentation-correctness only, not a behavior change with current content.

All three formulas now carry an explicit Owner column distinguishing what Hero System computes/emits from what Damage & Status computes, closing the ambiguity that let all three drift out of sync with D&S's actual authoritative pipeline.
Prior verdict resolved: Partially — pass 1's original blockers (hero_bonus_tier rename, tower_removed subscription) remain resolved and unaffected; this pass found 3 formulas (2 flagged, 1 found incidentally) that had silently drifted from D&S's ownership since D&S was authored after this GDD's pass 1.
